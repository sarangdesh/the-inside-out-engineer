# Contributing

Corrections, questions, clearer explanations, additional references, and substantive contributions are welcome.

Open an issue to report a problem or suggest a topic. For a correction, identify the relevant passage, explain the concern, and include supporting sources where possible. A topic suggestion is welcome, but does not imply a commitment or delivery date.

Pull requests are welcome for improvements to existing material. For a substantial new deep dive or major restructuring, open an issue first to establish scope and avoid duplicated effort.

Contributions should preserve technical depth, explain unfamiliar concepts, distinguish guarantees from implementation choices, and credit their sources. Submit only material you have the right to contribute.

Unless explicitly agreed otherwise, contributions are submitted under the repository licence. Contributors retain copyright in their contributions.
